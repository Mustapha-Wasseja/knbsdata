# knbsdata: Discover, Retrieve and Cite Official Kenyan Statistics

Provides a consistent interface for discovering and retrieving official
data and statistical products of the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics
(KNBS): its survey archive <https://statistics.knbs.or.ke/nada/>,
publications library, census, consumer price and economic data portals
<https://opendata.knbs.or.ke/beta/databrowser/>, the open FinAccess
survey microdata <https://finaccess.knbs.or.ke/>, and the Kenya
Demographic and Health Survey indicators published through the DHS
Program API <https://api.dhsprogram.com/>. Standardises them into
documented schemas, preserves provenance metadata such as release dates
and checksums, and generates reproducible citations. This is an
independent open-source project and is not an official KNBS product.
